The premise

Confront the brutal facts — early

The companies that pull away rarely have fewer problems. They face the hard truths of their situation sooner — a discipline Jim Collins named “confront the brutal facts” in Good to Great. This scan is built to do exactly that: surface where your first 100 days will leak while it is still cheap to fix.
